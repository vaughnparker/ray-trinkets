The Spike Ball Riddle

Imagine a ball with spikes sticking straight out of it, all the same length.

The spikes don't all lie flat in one plane.

It doesn't matter which spike you hold it by: it always looks exactly the same.

And if you twirl it around the spike you're holding, it matches itself again before you've gone a full turn.

How many different designs are there?

Have an idea? Put it on the test bench below. It checks each design against the riddle and tells you why it fails.
