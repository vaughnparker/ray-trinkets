ray-trinkets — the 7

Every elementary ray-set: a single orbit of dots under a polyhedral rotation group (T, O, or I). Pick one, then pick how the dots become a physical object.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Spike-ball is the dots as literal rays; vertex is their convex hull (dots become corners); face slides a tangent plane in at each dot until neighbors meet (dots become faces) — the dual solid.
