ray-trinkets — kissing flats

A round flat at every dot, all the same depth, cut until neighbouring flats just touch. The flats are the dots, the touching points are the edges, and the leftover curved patches are the faces of the vertex solid.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The slider grinds every flat deeper at once: past the kissing point the flats merge along edges, and at the end the ball is gone and only the face solid is left.
